The distinction between representation and relation is foundational to the interpretation of every operational consequence examined throughout this document.

Relation describes the invariant organization from which observable structure emerges.

Representation describes the form through which that organization becomes accessible to an observer or computational system.

The two are connected.

They are not identical.

The distinction may be expressed as:

Relation = Invariant Organization

Representation = Observable Encoding

Without this distinction, geometrical form, numerical value and machine output risk being mistaken for the generator that produced them.

RP9 explicitly rejects this reversal.

Representation requires an organization to represent.

Accordingly, relation must precede representation methodologically.
